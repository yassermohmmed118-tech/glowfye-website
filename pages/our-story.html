The Story of How We Got Started

January 2012

Glowfye is born from a passion for redefining beauty standards. With a commitment to authenticity and inclusivity, we set out to create a brand that celebrates individuality and self-expression.

January 2016

 A milestone year for Glowfye as we launch our debut skincare line, combining the latest innovations in skincare with the power of natural ingredients. Our goal is to provide effective, high-quality products that enhance the natural beauty of our customers.
